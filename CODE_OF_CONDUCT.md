# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Totpy to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use Totpy

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other channel the project uses) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@amit-y11](https://github.com/amit-y11), using
the contact details on their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept
confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose
behavior is harmful. Consequences depend on severity, from a private warning to a permanent ban.
